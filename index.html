01.A / Workforce

Employee scheduling apps

I built employee-facing apps for weekly schedule submissions, schedule changes, and late arrivals, along with tracking for the operations team.

Inside the workflow

A weekly schedule tells planners what to expect. A change request updates that plan; a late-arrival report flags a same-day exception. I built submission flows around those different needs so operations could track what had come in.

01.B / Service operations

Outages & service degradation

I built outage dashboards and tracking systems, plus a way to track degraded service and responses from affected employees.

What the team tracks

An outage and a slowdown raise different questions. The first is about service availability; the second also depends on what people are experiencing. Tracking employee responses gives the operations team another source of information alongside the incident record.

01.C / Business intelligence

Coverage & productivity reporting

I build performance dashboards, coverage reports, and SQL analyses for staffing and delivery teams.

A question behind the numbers

How much documentation work happens after hours? For the Dhaka team, I looked at note volume, active hours, and daily averages together. A higher note count alone doesn’t tell you whether people worked faster or simply worked longer.

# reporting
01.D / Implementation

Documentation quality & rollout

I helped pilot an AI-assisted documentation review tool, trained colleagues to interpret its exports, and troubleshot runs that surfaced unsupported patterns.

From pilot to everyday use

Part of my work was helping reviewers recognize when a suggested pattern wasn’t supported by the underlying documentation. I also helped define how to prioritize follow-up using feedback, audit results, and coverage information.
